# PHY-T1003: REX Sensor Placement Assessment

**Tactic:** PHY-TAC-03 (Portal & Architectural Barrier Protection)  
**Target Object:** SENSOR  
**Status:** Base Assessment Coverage  

## Assessment Hypothesis
Interior Request-to-Exit (REX) motion detectors whose detection cones extend to perimeter door margins may be susceptible to non-contact activation through door seams if directional shielding is absent.

## Observable Condition
The assessor establishes whether the interior REX passive infrared (PIR) sensor optical body or detection field is visible from the exterior side of the portal through transom, jamb, or threshold gaps without directional deflector shielding.

## Inspection Procedure (15 Seconds)
1. Inspect threshold, transom, and perimeter gaps around the closed door assembly.
2. Check for direct optical line-of-sight to the interior REX sensor.
3. Verify presence and orientation of directional deflector hoods or perimeter brush sweeps.

## Result Model
- **Hardened:** UL-listed directional deflector hood restricts PIR field strictly to interior egress zone.
- **Deficient:** Unshielded wide-angle PIR lens directly visible through perimeter door margins.
- **Condition Requires Validation:** Shroud installed but proximity to gap requires controlled trigger verification.

## Limitations
- **Can Establish:** Direct line-of-sight to sensor and presence of physical deflector hoods.
- **Cannot Establish:** Sensor internal sensitivity settings, pulse count logic, or verified bypass tripping without active environmental testing.
