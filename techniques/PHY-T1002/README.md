# PHY-T1002: Door & Latch Protection Assessment

**Tactic:** PHY-TAC-03 (Portal & Architectural Barrier Protection)  
**Target Object:** DOOR  
**Status:** Base Assessment Coverage  

## Assessment Hypothesis
Door and frame geometry may permit mechanical manipulation of the latch bolt assembly where protective physical shielding is absent and strike clearances exceed engineering tolerances.

## Observable Condition
The assessor evaluates whether an outward-swinging door has an exposed latch bolt visible through frame margins exceeding applicable engineering tolerances (e.g. 3.2mm / 1/8 in. under applicable NFPA 80 / SDI scope) without an overlapping protective astragal guard plate.

## Inspection Procedure (15 Seconds)
1. Determine door swing direction.
2. Insert a feeler gauge at the latch strike interface to measure perimeter margin.
3. Verify presence, material, and overlap of continuous stainless steel security astragal plate.

## Result Model
- **Hardened:** Continuous full-height astragal overlapping jamb; latch bolt fully shielded from direct mechanical access.
- **Deficient:** Latch bolt accessible with perimeter strike gap exceeding applicable specification (e.g. 3.2mm / 1/8 in.) and zero protective astragal.
- **Condition Requires Validation:** Margin exceeds 3.2mm on non-rated assembly where deadlocking auxiliary latch engagement requires physical verification.

## Limitations
- **Can Establish:** Perimeter strike clearance against standard tolerances and protective guard coverage.
- **Cannot Establish:** Lock cylinder pin resistance or interior deadbolt status.
