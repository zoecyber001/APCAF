# Standard Engagement Letter Consent Clause & Addendum

This document contains standardized legal and scope clauses to insert directly into Statements of Work (SOW), Master Services Agreements (MSA), or Assessment Authorization letters prior to physical site walks.

---

## 1. Single-Sentence Fast-Path Clause (Recommended for SOW Schedule A)

> *"Client explicitly authorizes the assessment team to perform non-invasive, passive radio frequency (RF) credential reads and non-intrusive passive physical network port link detection during the inspection to verify vendor hardware installation specifications."*

---

## 2. Expanded Scope & Non-Destructive Warranty Clause (Full Legal SOW Addendum)

For engagements requiring formal legal department sign-off, use the expanded text below:

```markdown
Section X: Physical Control Specification Verification (APCAF Testing)

X.1 Authorization & Scope:
Client explicitly authorizes the Assessor to perform non-destructive, passive verification of physical access control hardware specifications within designated scoped facilities. Testing is strictly limited to:
  (a) Contactless, passive radio frequency identification (RFID/NFC) protocol inspection to determine credential encryption standards;
  (b) Visual alignment, latch throw verification, and gap dimension inspection of perimeter barriers and interior secure zone doors;
  (c) Passive physical layer (Layer 1/2) network drop link-state testing via non-transmitting test instrumentation.

X.2 Zero Operational Impact & Fire-Safety Integrity:
All APCAF inspections are strictly non-invasive. The Assessor shall not:
  (a) Transmit malicious network packets or establish unauthenticated Layer 3 connections;
  (b) Modify, bypass, disassemble, or interfere with active life-safety, fire alarm, or emergency egress hardware;
  (c) Employ mechanical lock-picking, destructive entry, or credential cloning/replay devices.

X.3 Output & Defect Reporting:
Findings from this inspection shall be documented as a "Vendor Installation QA Scorecard" to assist the Client in validating vendor contract specifications and warranty obligations prior to final installation acceptance.
```

## 3. Pre-Engagement Checklist for Assessors

- [ ] Fast-path consent clause integrated into client-signed engagement letter.
