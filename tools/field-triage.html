BADGE PHY-T1001 Credential Technology Assessment
5s Check • Pocket RFID Reader
Step 2: What are you checking? Contactless passive carrier frequency. Does this credential rely on an exposed static UID without cryptographic mutual authentication?
Remediation Action: Replace credential stock with AES-128 crypto smartcards (DESFire EV3 / Seos) and activate SAM keys on readers.
DOOR PHY-T1002 Door & Latch Protection Assessment
15s Check • Visual & Feeler Gauge
Step 2: What are you checking? Latch bolt exposure and clearance margin. Does the strike margin exceed 3.2mm (1/8 in.) with direct line-of-sight to the latch bolt and no overlapping steel astragal guard?
Remediation Action: Install full-height continuous stainless steel security astragal plate on outward door assembly.
SENSOR PHY-T1003 REX Sensor Placement Assessment
15s Check • Visual Line-of-Sight
Step 2: What are you checking? Request-to-Exit (REX) PIR sensor coverage. Is the interior REX motion sensor optical field visible through transom or threshold gaps without a directional deflector hood?
Remediation Action: Mount UL-listed directional deflector hood over interior PIR motion sensor.
PORT PHY-T1004 Exposed Physical Network Interface Assessment
10s Check • Passive LED Link Tester
Step 2: What are you checking? Common area Ethernet drop state. Does an accessible wall jack in an unrestricted area broadcast an active physical Layer 1 carrier signal upon passive connection?
Remediation Action: Administratively shut down unmonitored switch ports at patch panel or configure 802.1X quarantine.

Audit Scope & Consent Clause

Single-Sentence Fast-Path Clause

"Client explicitly authorizes the assessment team to perform non-invasive, passive radio frequency (RF) credential reads and non-intrusive passive physical network port link detection during the inspection to verify vendor hardware installation specifications."

Scope & Life-Safety Protection Guarantees

• Zero Operational Impact: Strictly non-intrusive reads only. No packet injection or sniffing.
• Life-Safety Non-Interference: Zero manipulation of fire alarm, emergency egress, or crash bar hardware.
• Contractual Purpose: Generates a quality assurance scorecard to assist client in validating contractor warranty deliverables.
