Architecture Overview

APCAF Specification & Philosophy

The Adversarial Physical Control Assessment Framework (APCAF) provides a standardized method for assessing observable physical conditions associated with known adversarial bypass techniques through defined, repeatable, and non-destructive inspection procedures.

Traditional compliance audits verify nominal control presence on paper (verifying that a badge reader or door lock exists). APCAF complements audits by testing whether the physical installation withstands known bypass conditions: "What physical condition would permit bypass, and does the installed hardware prevent that condition?"

Framework Governance

Operational Boundaries

To preserve scientific integrity and prevent misinterpretation, APCAF strictly bounds its operational scope:

What APCAF Does:

• Maps known adversary bypass techniques to observable physical hardware conditions.
• Establishes repeatable, non-invasive inspection procedures and benchmark budgets.
• Documents observable hardware deficiencies against engineering specifications and building codes.
• Derives deterministic risk context based on Asset Criticality, Physical Exposure, and Consequence.
• Supports facility commissioning, vendor warranty verification, and quality assurance.

What APCAF Does NOT Do:

• Does NOT perform destructive penetration testing, lockpicking, or forced entry.
• Does NOT prove successful exploitation or claim a demonstrated physical breach.
• Does NOT certify regulatory compliance (PCI DSS, ISO 27001, SOC 2 belong to formal audits).
• Does NOT determine contractual or legal liability automatically.

Separation of Concerns

The 5 Disciplinary Layers of APCAF

APCAF decouples threat intelligence from physical inspection, asset context, and executive treatment decisions:

01. Technique
What can an adversary potentially do? Catalogued across Tactics PHY-TAC-01 through PHY-TAC-06 in canonical YAML specifications.
02. Procedure
What can we safely observe to test the condition? Standardized non-invasive inspection methods, tools, and read times.
03. Finding
What did we actually observe? Empirical evidence, measurements, photos, and technical disposition (Hardened, Deficient, Condition Requires Validation).
04. Risk Context
What does that mean in this specific asset environment? Deterministic calculation: Asset Criticality × Physical Exposure × Consequence.
05. Treatment
What does the organization decide to do? Defensible action: Remediate, Compensate, Accept, or Avoid.
Operational Authorization

Assessment Authorization Language

APCAF inspections require zero destructive tools and zero life-safety interference. Organizations may incorporate this standard clause into Statements of Work (SOW), master service agreements, or audit charters: 

"Client explicitly authorizes the assessment team to perform non-invasive physical security
control evaluations, passive radio frequency (RF) credential reads, and non-intrusive physical
network port link detection during the inspection to verify vendor hardware installation specifications."

Notice: This is an operational template, not legal advice. Organizations should adapt it with qualified legal counsel.

Attack Surface Matrix

The 6 Physical Security Tactics

Physical security controls protect facility spatial boundaries across six defined tactical objectives:

Tactic ID Tactic Name Tactical Objective
PHY-TAC-01 Physical Reconnaissance Gathering spatial, optical, or RF intelligence prior to physical approach.
PHY-TAC-02 Perimeter & Ingress Breaching or bypassing external site boundaries, vehicle gates, or exterior pedestrian portals.
PHY-TAC-03 Credential & Access Bypass Circumventing electronic access-control authenticators, RFID readers, or key management.
PHY-TAC-04 Interior Traversal & Evasion Traversing interior secure zones and evading detection within facilities.
PHY-TAC-05 Physical-to-Cyber Execution Gaining unauthorized logical or data access via exposed physical hardware interfaces.
PHY-TAC-06 Egress & Cleanup Exiting the facility and concealing physical evidence of unauthorized presence.
Field Methodology

Fast-Path Benchmark Budgets

The APCAF Fast-Path Profile defines targeted time budgets for baseline checks during physical walks:

5 Seconds

15 Seconds

15 Seconds

10 Seconds
PHY-T1004 (Network Port): Passive LED link-state test to verify administrative port shutdown or isolation.
PHY-TAC-03: Credential & Access Bypass

PHY-T1001: Credential Technology Assessment

Base Coverage
Assessment Hypothesis

Physical credentials broadcasting static, unauthenticated identifiers may permit harvest, replay, or cloning where the access-control architecture does not enforce cryptographic challenge-response mutual authentication.

Evaluation Dimension Assessment Criteria
Technology Identified Interrogate carrier frequency (125 kHz Low Frequency vs 13.56 MHz High Frequency) and chip protocol standard.
Authentication Mechanism Determine whether credential broadcasts a static identifier (UID/CSN) without requiring a cryptographic handshake.
Behavioral Assessment Distinguish static unauthenticated broadcast from dynamic session-based cryptographic challenge-response transactions.
Verification Boundary Record whether reader-side cryptographic key enforcement (SAM configuration, AES mutual auth) requires administrative configuration audit.
Canonical Technique Specification (YAML)
id: "PHY-T1001"
name: "Credential Technology Assessment"
tactic_id: "PHY-TAC-03"
target_object: "BADGE"
target_time_seconds: 5
hypothesis: "Physical credentials broadcasting static, unauthenticated identifiers may permit harvest, replay, or cloning where the access-control architecture does not enforce cryptographic challenge-response mutual authentication."
assessment_procedure:
  method: "passive_rf_interrogation"
  tools_required: ["Pocket dual-frequency RFID/NFC interrogation tool"]
result_model:
  hardened_criteria: "Credential architecture implements mutual cryptographic challenge-response authentication with verified reader-side key enforcement."
  deficient_criteria: "Credential relies on unencrypted static identifier broadcast (125 kHz Prox, EM4100, or unauthenticated 13.56 MHz CSN) susceptible to passive harvest and replay."
  condition_requires_validation_criteria: "High-frequency smartcard observed; reader-side cryptographic challenge-response, SAM configuration, and anti-downgrade policies require administrative verification."
mitigation_id: "PHY-M1001"
technical_references:
  - standard: "ISO/IEC 14443-4"
    section: "Transmission Protocol"
  - standard: "NIST SP 800-116 Rev 1"
    section: "Section 4.1"

Mathematical Specification

Deterministic Risk Scoring Engine

APCAF strictly rejects rigid universal checklist mandates. Risk is derived deterministically from three orthogonal physical parameters:

Risk Score = Asset Criticality (AC: 1..4) × Physical Exposure (PE: 1..4) × Consequence (C: 1..4)
Score Range Calculated Risk Level Governance Action & Eligibility
1 - 8 LOW Eligible for standard operational risk acceptance with documented rationale.
9 - 23 MEDIUM Requires formal review. Eligible for validated compensating controls or scheduled remediation.
24 - 47 HIGH Mandates prioritized remediation or high-assurance compensating detection with SLA.
48 - 64 CRITICAL Immediate executive escalation, active physical guard post, and remediation hold.
Decision Governance

The 4 Risk Treatment Options

1. REMEDIATE

Direct physical hardware modification satisfying the canonical mitigation specification (`PHY-MXXXX`).

2. COMPENSATE

Deploy a secondary physical barrier or immediate tamper sensor when primary remediation is structurally constrained.

3. ACCEPT

Named business risk owner accepts residual risk within documented organizational appetite with an annual expiration date.

4. AVOID / REMOVE

Eliminate the physical threat surface entirely by decommissioning unneeded ports or walling off redundant openings.

Ground-Truth Proof

Three Canonical Reference Cases

Demonstrating how identical physical observations yield three distinct, defensible outcomes based on context:

Scenario Observation 3-Axis Calculation Appetite Defensible Decision
A. Mop Closet 5.2 mm gap, latch exposed, zero astragal AC-1 × PE-3 × C-1 = 3 (LOW) MEDIUM (Pass) ACCEPT RISK
B. HR Records 4.8 mm gap, historic glass frame AC-3 × PE-2 × C-3 = 18 (MEDIUM) LOW (Breach) COMPENSATE (BMS + Beam)
C. Core Datacenter 5.2 mm gap, server suite double door AC-4 × PE-2 × C-4 = 32 (HIGH) LOW (Breach) REMEDIATE (WF-01 Warranty Hold)

Open Specification

Governance & Contribution Process

APCAF is governed by the APCAF Maintainers under an open Request for Comments (RFC) model. Community proposals for new tactics, techniques, or schema profiles must satisfy three mandatory criteria:

1. Strictly Non-Invasive: Must define repeatable non-destructive observation procedures.
2. Zero Life-Safety Interference: Must never manipulate emergency egress, panic hardware, or fire alarms.
3. Defensible Citations: Grounded in authoritative architectural, fire, or electrical engineering standards.

Engagement Addendum

Audit Scope & Consent Clause

Single-Sentence Fast-Path Clause

"Client explicitly authorizes the assessment team to perform non-invasive physical security control evaluations, passive radio frequency (RF) credential reads, and non-intrusive physical network port link detection during the inspection to verify vendor hardware installation specifications."

Scope & Life-Safety Protection Guarantees

• Zero Operational Impact: Strictly non-intrusive reads only. No packet injection or sniffing.
• Life-Safety Non-Interference: Zero manipulation of fire alarm, emergency egress, or crash bar hardware.
• Contractual Purpose: Generates a quality assurance scorecard to assist client in validating contractor deliverables.
