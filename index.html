APCAF BASE v0.1.0 OPEN PHYSICAL SECURITY SPECIFICATION

Physical security deserves more than a checklist.

APCAF is an open specification for systematically assessing physical security controls against adversarial bypass techniques, using observable evidence, structured evaluations, and organization-defined risk appetite.

Methodology Chain

From Physical Observation to Defensible Action

A rigorous 6-stage lifecycle linking adversary hypotheses to organizational risk treatment.

01
Technique
What can an adversary attempt? (e.g. Latch Slip).
02
Observation
What does the assessor directly measure? (e.g. 5.2mm gap).
03
Evidence
What objective data proves the condition? (e.g. photo/gauge).
04
Disposition
Hardened, Deficient, or Requires Validation.
05
Risk Context
Deterministic score: Asset Criticality × Exposure × Impact.
06
Treatment
Remediate, Compensate, Accept, or Avoid.

Engagement Addendum

Audit Scope & Consent Clause

Single-Sentence Fast-Path Clause

"Client explicitly authorizes the assessment team to perform non-invasive, passive radio frequency (RF) credential reads and non-intrusive passive physical network port link detection during the inspection to verify vendor hardware installation specifications."

Scope & Life-Safety Protection Guarantees

• Zero Operational Impact: Strictly non-intrusive reads only. No packet injection or sniffing.
• Life-Safety Non-Interference: Zero manipulation of fire alarm, emergency egress, or crash bar hardware.
• Contractual Purpose: Generates a quality assurance scorecard to assist client in validating contractor warranty deliverables.
